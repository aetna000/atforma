I've drafted the end-to-end AWS pipeline. Synthetic devices send to AWS IoT Core, which feeds a replayable Kinesis stream. A validator checks events against a device registry and rejects unknown devices or invalid units, then a scorer deduplicates events, applies the late-event policy and calls a SageMaker endpoint. Each result is stored with its evidence and model version, and anomalies alert an operator through SNS.

**First local test:** we'd replay a fixed set of synthetic events through the validator and scorer, using a scripted fake model. That test can show:
- unknown devices and invalid units are rejected
- duplicate events produce exactly one result
- late and out-of-order events follow the policy
- replay after a disconnect recovers the missing results
- each anomaly result names its evidence and model version

It proves the pipeline's behaviour, not detection quality. Testing the real model needs held-out synthetic data, measured miss and false-alarm rates, and a reachable endpoint.
